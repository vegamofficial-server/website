Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vasudev & Sons Technologies Private Limited (CIN U62099KL2026PTC106936), registered office at Building No. 303, NSS Building, Kappu, Madakkathanam, Muvattupuzha, Ernakulam – 686670, Kerala ("Vegam", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Vegam app or www.vegamservices.com (the "Platform").

Under the DPDP Act, Vegam is the Data Fiduciary for your data, and you are the Data Principal. Terms not defined here have the meaning given in our Terms of Service.

Our privacy promises in short:

  • We collect only what we need to arrange your home service safely.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • Your address and phone number go to the assigned Service Professional only for your booking.
  • We never store your full card details.
  • You can see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent, anytime.

This policy covers Customers. Service Professionals receive a separate privacy notice when they join.

2. How we collect it

  1. From you, when you sign up, book, pay, chat with support, rate a service or answer a survey.
  2. Automatically, through the app and website, using cookies, SDKs and server logs (see Section 12).
  3. From others: our payment partner (payment status), Service Professionals (visit notes, incident reports, ratings of you), and anyone who books a service for you at your address.

3. If you don't share your data

Some data is essential to deliver a home service: your name, mobile number and service address. Without it we cannot take your booking. Optional data (email, photo, gender) is not required, and you can use the app without it.

4. How we use your data

We use your personal data only for these purposes:

  1. Creating and verifying your account (OTP by SMS or WhatsApp)
  2. Taking, scheduling and delivering your bookings, including assigning a Service Professional
  3. Processing payments, refunds and GST invoices
  4. Sending booking confirmations, reminders, plan renewals and service updates
  5. Customer support, complaints, the quality guarantee and damage claims
  6. Safety: verifying identities, handling SOS alerts and investigating incidents
  7. Preventing fraud, misuse of offers and abuse of Service Professionals
  8. Improving the app and our services, using anonymised or aggregated data where possible
  9. Sending offers and promotions, only if you opt in
  10. Meeting legal obligations, enforcing our Terms and responding to lawful requests from authorities

We will not use your data for any new purpose without telling you and, where required, asking for your consent again.

5. Consent

  1. When you sign up, we ask for your consent to process your data for the purposes above. We show this notice in plain language, and you can read it in English or [Malayalam].
  2. Marketing messages need a separate consent, which is off by default.
  3. You can withdraw consent anytime in the app (Settings → Privacy) or by writing to vegamofficial@gmail.com. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent. After withdrawal we stop processing, except where the law requires us to keep data (Section 9). If you withdraw consent for essential data, we will not be able to provide bookings.
  4. Some processing does not need consent under the DPDP Act, such as complying with a court order or responding to a medical emergency.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by writing to vegamofficial@gmail.com. We process the request within 7 working days. Any active plan is cancelled and refunded under our Refund Policy. After deletion we erase your personal data, except what we must keep by law (Section 9).

10. Security and data breaches

  1. We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls (only staff who need it can see it), OTP-based login, [phone-number masking], regular backups and activity logs.
  2. Service Professionals see your address and phone number only during an active booking.
  3. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, as the DPDP Act requires, explaining what happened, the likely impact and the steps you can take.
  4. Keep your OTP private. Vegam will never ask for it.

11. Payments

Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments India Private Limited ("Cashfree"), an RBI-authorised payment aggregator that complies with PCI-DSS, the card industry's security standard. Your card number, CVV and UPI PIN are entered on the payment partner's secure screen and are never stored by Vegam. We receive only the payment status, transaction ID, method and last 4 digits of the card. The payment partner's own privacy policy also applies.

12. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses cookies, and our app uses similar tools (SDKs), to keep you logged in, remember preferences, understand how the Platform is used and fix problems. We use [Google Analytics / Firebase] for analytics and crash reports. We do not let advertising networks track you across other apps without your consent. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the website may stop working.

13. Children

The Platform is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Information about children in your home (for example, "a toddler is at home") should be shared only when needed for the Service Professional's work and safety.

14. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on secure servers of [hosting provider] located in [India / region]. If any data is processed outside India, we do so only as permitted under the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India, with the same level of protection.

15. Reviews and content you post

Ratings and reviews you post may be visible to Service Professionals and, in summary form, to other users. Do not include personal details such as phone numbers or addresses in reviews.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. We will notify you in the app or by SMS/email before any material change takes effect, and ask for fresh consent where the law requires it. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
