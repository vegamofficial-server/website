Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Effective from: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how payments, cancellations and refunds work for bookings made on the Vegam app and website (the "Platform"), operated by Vasudev & Sons Technologies Private Limited ("Vegam", "we", "us"). It forms part of our Terms of Service. Words not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms.

In short:

  • Cancel a one-time booking for free up to 4 hours before the visit.
  • Cancelling later, or no access to your home, costs 50% of the booking.
  • If we cancel or can't send a Service Professional, you get a full refund.
  • Monthly and weekly plans: cancel anytime and get the unused part refunded.
  • Refunds reach your original payment method in 3–5 working days.

1. Payment

1.1. You pay in advance in the app through our payment partner, Cashfree Payments, by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. Before you confirm, the app shows the full price and its breakup (service fee, platform fee, optional materials and GST where applicable). There are no hidden charges.

1.2. Pay only through the app. Payments made directly to a Service Professional are not covered by this policy.

1.3. If money is debited but your booking is not confirmed, the full amount is refunded automatically.

3. Rescheduling

3.1. You can move a booking to another available slot for free up to 4 hours before the visit starts. Rescheduling later than that is treated as a late cancellation (section 2).

3.2. Once your booking is confirmed, Vegam does not reschedule it on you. If something outside our control stops a visit (for example floods, heavy rain, strikes or government orders), we will offer you a new slot or a full refund, as you prefer.

4. If Vegam cancels or can't serve you

4.1. If your assigned Service Professional becomes unavailable, we will try to send a replacement. If we can't, you can choose another slot or a full refund.

4.2. If Vegam cancels a booking for any reason other than your conduct (see section 7), you get a full refund.

5. Monthly and weekly plans

5.1. Plans are paid in advance for the plan period. You can cancel a plan at any time in the app.

5.2. When you cancel, we refund the unused part of what you paid, worked out pro-rata on the visits left in the plan period: amount paid × (visits remaining ÷ total visits in the plan). Visits already completed, and visits cancelled late under section 2, count as used.

5.3. If you joined with a discount or launch offer, the refund is based on the amount you actually paid.

5.4. Skip days and renewal rules follow the plan terms shown when you buy the plan. We notify you before a plan renews, and you can cancel before renewal at no charge.

5.5. If you delete your account, any active plan is cancelled and the unused part is refunded under this section.

6. Quality issues

6.1. Not happy with the work? Report it in the app within 24 hours of the visit, with details or photos.

6.2. After checking, we will arrange a free re-do of the affected work, or give a partial or full refund, depending on what went wrong.

6.3. Accidental damage to your property is covered separately under our damage cover (up to ₹5,000 per booking). See section 8 of the Terms of Service.

7. When a refund isn't due

  • The Service Professional stops or refuses a visit because the home is unsafe, or because of abusive, threatening, harassing or inappropriate behaviour (Terms section 6.3).
  • The service was paid for outside the app.
  • A quality issue was reported more than 24 hours after the visit.
  • The account shows a pattern of false complaints or refund abuse (Terms section 6.6).

8. How refunds are paid

8.1. Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method within 3–5 working days. Your bank or card issuer may take a little longer to show it.

8.2. We'll tell you in the app or by SMS when a refund is processed, along with the reference number.

8.3. Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank, so we can fix the problem quickly.

9. How to cancel or ask for a refund

Cancel or reschedule in the app. For a refund question, use in-app chat, email vegamofficial@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp 9995588303. We acknowledge every request within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact our Grievance Officer (see the Terms of Service). Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
