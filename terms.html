1. Our services and Vegam's role

1.1. Vegam is a technology platform for booking home services, starting with housekeeping and maid services, delivered by trained and verified workers ("Service Professionals") at your home. We currently serve the Kakkanad / Infopark area of Kochi. The app shows the areas and services available to you.

1.2. Who provides the service. Most Service Professionals are independent partners who choose to offer their services through Vegam. Some team members, such as supervisors and trainers, are employed by Vegam. Independent partners are not employees or agents of Vegam and cannot create obligations on our behalf. For bookings delivered by independent partners, Vegam acts as an intermediary under the Information Technology Act, 2000: we arrange the booking, collect payment on their behalf, and handle support.

1.3. What Vegam does for every booking. Whoever delivers your service, Vegam verifies identity and background, trains every Service Professional, provides uniforms, ID cards, tools and equipment, sets service standards, and offers the quality guarantee and damage cover in Section 8.

1.4. Booking types. You can book (a) one-time or hourly visits, (b) weekly plans on fixed days, or (c) daily monthly plans. Plans are paid in advance for the plan period and follow the plan terms shown at purchase (number of visits, skip days, renewal), in addition to these Terms.

1.5. Matching. We assign Service Professionals based on availability, skill, location and ratings, partly using automated systems. Assignment is not a guarantee of a specific person, except where your plan says you will have a regular Service Professional.

1.6. Communications. We will contact you by SMS, phone, email, WhatsApp or app notification about bookings, payments and service updates. Promotional messages are sent only with your consent, and you can opt out anytime.

1.7. We may decline a booking for safety, legal or operational reasons. We may also add, change or stop services and features. We will give reasonable notice where a change affects a plan you have already paid for, and refund the unused part if we stop it.

1.8. The Platform is for personal and household use. Businesses booking for their premises should contact us first.
